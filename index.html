About Brew Haven

A third place between home and everywhere else.

Brew Haven started with one goal: bring people together over a really good cup of coffee. We buy beans from farms we know by name and bake in small batches every morning.

  • Ethically sourced beans, roasted for flavor not for shelf life
  • Quiet nooks for studying, big tables for barkada catch-ups
  • Loyalty beans on every order, redeemable for free drinks
